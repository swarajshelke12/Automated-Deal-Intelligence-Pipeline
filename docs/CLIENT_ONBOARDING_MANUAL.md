1. **Portal Sign-In**: Enter developer-provided Client ID and Access Key.
2. **Google Calendar Linking**: Input authorized Google email and OAuth credentials.
3. **AI & Research Keys**: Paste private Groq or OpenAI and Apify tokens.

5. **Developer Hand-Off**: Click Save & Continue. Engineers at nayva tech industries connect credentials to the backend n8n worker.
